Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Zapix ("Zapix", "we", "us") is a free live TV player that lists public streams from around the world. This policy explains what information is used when you open the Zapix website or app. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Zapix does not run advertising and does not sell your information.
  • Your favorites and settings stay on your own device.

2. Information stored on your device

To work properly, Zapix saves a few things in your browser's local storage (or in the app's storage). This includes your favorite channels, recently watched channels, your chosen theme, and player settings such as volume or video quality. This information never leaves your device through Zapix, and you can erase it at any time by clearing your browser data or the app's data. If you do, your favorites and settings will be lost.

3. Information collected automatically by others

Zapix does not host any video. When you watch a channel, your device connects directly to the server that provides that stream, and when channel logos appear, your device loads them from the servers that host them. Like any website, those servers can see technical details such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the time of your request. We do not control these servers and they have their own privacy practices.

The website also loads resources from these services, which may receive your IP address and basic browser details:

  • Google Fonts for the text fonts.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs for the video playback library (hls.js).
  • Google Cast software, only in Chrome and other Chromium browsers, to let you send a channel to a Chromecast. If you use casting, the stream address and channel name are sent to your Cast device.

The company that hosts the Zapix website may also keep ordinary server logs (for example IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

4. How we use information

The information described above is used only to make Zapix work: to play channels, remember your favorites and settings, and let you cast to your TV. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile of you, or show you targeted ads.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party services listed above as part of loading the site and playing streams, or if the law requires it.

6. Children

Zapix is a general audience service and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some channels contain content intended for adults, and parents should supervise what children watch.

7. Your choices

  • Clear your browser or app data to remove favorites and settings.
  • Use your browser's privacy settings or a VPN if you do not want streaming servers to see your IP address.
  • Stop using Zapix at any time.

8. Security

Because Zapix holds no accounts or personal profiles, there is very little for us to protect. Still, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and some channel streams are served by third parties over unencrypted connections that we do not control.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continuing to use Zapix after a change means you accept the updated policy.
