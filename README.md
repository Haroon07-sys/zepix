⚡ Zapix - Live TV from around the world
Free · No sign-up

Zapix is a fast, clean IPTV player that brings you live TV from around the world. Search every channel at once, jump to any channel by its number, and fall asleep with a timer. Watch on your Android phone or right here in your browser.

✨ Features
Everything you need to find and watch, built around speed and simplicity.

🔍 Search every country: One search box looks through all countries at once, with the best matches first.

🔢 Channel numbers: Every channel has a number. Type it to zap straight there, or press L to go back.

🌙 Sleep timer: Pick 15 minutes to 1.5 hours and Zapix stops playing by itself.

🗣️ Language filter: Narrow channels down to the language you want to watch.

❤️ Favorites: Tap the heart to keep a channel. Your favorites and recent channels stay on your device.

📺 Cast to your TV: On the web version in Chrome or Edge, send a channel to your Chromecast.

📥 Get Zapix
Install in under a minute. Use the Android app, or just open the web version on any device. No account needed.

Web Version
Open zapix.netlify.app in any modern browser.
On iPhone or iPad: Tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen for quick access.

Android App
Download the app: Get the Zapix file.

Allow the install: Open the file. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

Start watching: Open Zapix from your app list, pick a country and tap a channel.

❓ FAQ
Is Zapix really free?
Yes. Zapix is free, and does not ask you to sign up.

Where do the channels come from?
Zapix does not host any video. It lists channels that are publicly available on the internet and plays them straight from their own servers. Channel information comes from the community IPTV-org directory.

Why does a channel not play?
Channels can go offline, move, or be blocked in some countries at any time. If one does not work, try another channel or check again later.

Why does Android warn me when I install?
The app is installed from a file rather than from an app store, so Android asks for your permission first. You can turn that permission off again after installing.

Is there an iPhone version?
Not a native app yet, but the web version works well in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, to keep it on your home screen like an app.

Can I cast to my TV?
Yes, on the web version in Chrome, Edge, or other Chromium browsers, using the Cast button in the player. Some channels block casting.

What do you store about me?
Only your favorites and settings, and they stay on your own device.
